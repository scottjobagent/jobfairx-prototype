About this role

Baylor Scott & White is hiring Nurse Practitioners to join its outpatient primary care teams across the Dallas–Fort Worth area. You will carry your own patient panel, working alongside physicians, registered nurses and medical assistants to deliver day-to-day primary and preventive care.

This role is being filled through a JobFairX hiring event. Interviews for every candidate happen on the same day, and you choose the time that suits you.

What you'll do

  • Assess, diagnose and treat patients across a general primary care panel.
  • Order and interpret diagnostic tests, and prescribe treatment within your scope of practice.
  • Develop care plans with patients and follow them through subsequent visits.
  • Refer to specialists where care falls outside primary care, and coordinate that handover.
  • Keep clear, timely clinical documentation in the electronic health record.
  • Work with the wider care team on preventive screening and chronic disease management.

What you'll bring

  • An active Nurse Practitioner licence in the state of Texas, or eligibility to obtain one.
  • National certification as an FNP, AGNP or equivalent.
  • A Master of Science in Nursing or Doctor of Nursing Practice.
  • Current BLS certification.
  • Two or more years of clinical experience is preferred, though new graduates are considered.

Pay and benefits

  • $118,000 – $132,000 a year, depending on experience.
  • Medical, dental and vision cover from your first month.
  • 401(k) with employer matching.
  • Paid time off and paid holidays.
  • Continuing education allowance and licence renewal paid.
