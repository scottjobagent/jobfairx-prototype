Developer review notes

Homepage design updates in this prototype

Remove before launch
  • Hero atmosphereThe subtle blue glow is in the upper-right of the hero background. Keep the navigation itself clean white.
  • How it works layersEach of the four workflow examples has its own soft circle treatment behind the card: blue, mint, gold, then blue.
  • Workflow connector removedRemove the scrolling vertical line and progress dots between steps. The four steps should stand independently.
  • Interview format lives in step threeKeep In person, Video, and Phone inside the existing workflow as the Event day example, not as a separate standalone section.
  • Scheduling is a video momentThe flexible interview scheduling section uses a clean 16:9 video placeholder. Replace it with the scheduling walkthrough when ready.
  • Protect the clean decision areasDo not add more texture to results, pricing, or testimonials. Those sections should remain calm and easy to scan.
