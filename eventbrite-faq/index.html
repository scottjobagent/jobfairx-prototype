Listing FAQ for the United States and Canada

The eleven questions and answers that go on every JobFairX employer listing on Eventbrite. One set for US events and one for Canadian events.

United States FAQCanada FAQ

The two sets differ in one answer

Question 6, “Are candidates local to the city where the event is held?” Everything else is word for word the same.

United StatesYes, candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city, so you’re connecting with local, relevant talent.
CanadaYes, candidates are verified to be within approximately 32 kilometres of the event city, so you’re connecting with local, relevant talent.

United States FAQ

On all 19 published US listings today.

  1. 1

    Where do interviews take place?

    You choose. When you set up your jobs, you select your interview location: in person at your address, on JobFairX video, or by phone.

  2. 2

    Do I need a booth or table?

    No. There is no venue, booth, or table. You interview candidates in person at your address, on JobFairX video, or by phone.

  3. 3

    How does candidate matching and interviewing work?

    Once you post your open jobs, candidate matching activates immediately and you will start receiving interview requests within a few hours. You'll receive a notification for each request and a summary email every morning at 9:00 AM. You can review candidate résumés before accepting or declining.

  4. 4

    How many interviews can I expect?

    It depends on your plan. Starter employers (1 job per event) average around 20 interviews. Growth employers (up to 3 jobs) average around 60. Pro employers (up to 6 jobs) typically see 100+.

  5. 5

    How many recruiters can take part?

    Starter includes 2 recruiter seats. Growth includes up to 5 recruiter seats. Pro includes unlimited recruiters.

  6. 6

    Are candidates local to the city where the event is held?

    Yes, candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city, so you’re connecting with local, relevant talent.

    Different from Canada

  7. 7

    How are candidates sourced?

    JobFairX has a database of more than 3 million job seekers, with over 2,200 new registrations each day. Candidates register for specific events by city and date. Once you post your jobs, we match your roles with registered candidates and invite qualified matches to request an interview.

  8. 8

    How are candidates prepared for interviews?

    Candidates receive pre-event preparation materials, including interview tips, technical requirements along with guidance on presenting themselves professionally. They also receive automated emails and SMS leading up to their scheduled interview to ensure high attendance rates.

  9. 9

    What happens after I register?

    You receive a welcome message from JobFairX with a link to your dashboard. There you post your jobs and set your interview settings. Candidate matching is activated automatically.

  10. 10

    What is the refund policy?

    There are no refunds.

  11. 11

    Who do I contact with questions?

    Email eventbrite@jobfairx.com.
