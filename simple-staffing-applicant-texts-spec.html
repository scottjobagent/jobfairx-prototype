Simple Staffing Applicant Texts Spec

Three texts from Simple Staffing ask every Indeed applicant to request an interview time on JobFairX. This page carries the exact templates, every merge field, the send schedule and the rules a developer needs to build it.

01

The three texts

Line breaks are part of the template. Straight apostrophes and plain hyphens only.

Text 1

Simple Staffing: We received your application for the {Job title} job on Indeed. {Company} would like to interview you on {Interview date} between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM {Time zone}. {Format sentence}
{Request line}
Reply STOP if you no longer want to receive texts from Simple Staffing.

Text 2

Simple Staffing: {Company} would still like to interview you for the {Job title} job you applied for on Indeed. Interview times are open for {Interview date} between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM {Time zone}. {Format sentence}
{Request line}
Reply STOP if you no longer want to receive texts from Simple Staffing.

Text 3

Simple Staffing: {Company} is still scheduling interviews for the {Job title} job you applied for on Indeed. Times on {Interview date} between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM {Time zone} are open until they fill or the interviews begin. {Format sentence}
{Request line}
Reply STOP if you no longer want to receive texts from Simple Staffing.

03

Send schedule

All send times run on the applicant's own clock. Every send is checked at the moment it is handed to Twilio, not when it was scheduled: the person has not requested a time or opted out, slots are still open, the interviews have not begun, and the job is not cancelled.

TextWhenWindow
Text 1Planned send = the moment the application reaches the automation, plus 15 minutes. If the planned send falls inside the window, send then. Otherwise send at the first 8:00 AM after it, spread at random over 8:00 to 8:30 AM so a held batch does not go out in one burst.8:00 AM to 8:45 PM applicant-local; Oregon numbers 8:00 AM to 7:45 PM
Text 210:00 AM applicant-local on the calendar day after Text 1's planned day, spread over 10:00 to 10:30. Never on the interview day.Same window
Text 310:00 AM applicant-local on the calendar day two days before the interview date (counted on the job's calendar), spread over 10:00 to 10:30. Skipped when that day is the same as, or earlier than, Text 2's day. Never on the interview day.Same window
  • Applicant time zone: from the state or ZIP on the application; if missing, from the phone's area code; if the area code spans two zones or is not geographic, use the job's time zone. Store time zones by IANA name, never as a fixed offset, so daylight time and Arizona work.
  • Oregon: applies when the application's state is Oregon or the area code is 503, 971, 541 or 458. Window ends 7:45 PM; at most 3 automated texts to one number in any 24 hours.
  • Twilio: hand every message over with ValidityPeriod 900 seconds, so a queued text can never arrive after 9:00 PM (8:00 PM Oregon).
  • Interview day: nothing is sent except a Text 1 to someone who applies that morning while requests are open and at least one hour remains before the closing moment.

04

05

Replies, STOP and HELP

Nobody answers replies by text. The system handles opt-outs and HELP itself; every other reply is forwarded to an email address and checked weekly.

  • Keep Twilio's automatic opt-out handling on with its full keyword set (STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE, OPTOUT). A keyword reply blocks the number at once across all jobs and cancels its queued texts.
  • Twilio only acts on single-word replies, so the app also checks every inbound message: if it contains stop, quit, end, revoke, opt out, optout, cancel or unsubscribe, in any case, with any punctuation, anywhere in the message, block the number the same way.
  • Every other reply is forwarded to the replies mailbox with the applicant's name, job, employer and the reply text. Someone reads that mailbox at least weekly. A plain request to stop ("stop texting me", "wrong number", "not interested") is entered as an opt-out within ten business days, as the FCC rule requires. No question is answered by text.
  • One block list serves every sending number. The follow-up check reads this list, not the literal word STOP.
  • HELP auto-reply (required at registration): Simple Staffing: texts about jobs you applied for. Help: {support email or phone}. Reply STOP to opt out. Scott supplies the contact.
  • After an opt-out, one confirmation text within 5 minutes is allowed, then nothing: Simple Staffing: You're opted out and won't get more texts from us. Scott confirms whether to send it (decision 5).

06

Characters and length

One character outside the GSM-7 set turns a 3-segment text into 6. Job titles and company names from Workable often carry curly quotes and en dashes, so every merge value is normalised before the template is filled.

  • Curly single quotes to a straight apostrophe; curly double quotes to a straight quote; en dash, em dash and minus sign to a hyphen; ellipsis character to three periods; non-breaking and other Unicode spaces to a normal space.
  • Emoji and any other character outside GSM-7 are removed. Accented letters inside GSM-7 (for example é) stay.
  • Log any message that would still send as UCS-2, and keep the templates themselves to straight apostrophes and hyphens.
  • Measured with the sample values and a 30-character link: Text 1 is 383 characters (video and phone) or 411 (in person); Text 2 is 402 or 430; Text 3 is 428 or 456. All are 3 segments. Text 3 for an in-person job reaches 4 segments when the title and company are both long (473 with a 39-character title and 30-character company). Non-Indeed versions are 10 characters shorter.
  • Test-send one message with the company "Baylor Scott & White Health" and confirm the ampersand arrives intact; some platforms escape it.

07

Twilio registration and the link

Carriers compare live traffic with the registered campaign, so the registration changes before the templates do.

  • Replace the campaign's sample messages with the three new texts, including the real link domain, before switching. The brand named in the texts (Simple Staffing) must match the registered brand.
  • Set the HELP message and the opt-out message on the campaign to the lines in section 5.
  • The link uses a domain JobFairX controls, never a public shortener such as bit.ly. Keep the whole link to 39 characters or fewer, including https://.
  • The landing page shows the employer name and the job title above the fold, so the applicant sees the same facts the text gave them before anything about JobFairX.

08

Logging

Record these from the first send so the request rate can be reported every week and split by job, employer, format and text version.

Per text sent: application, text number (1, 2 or 3), template version, planned send time, actual send time in UTC and in the applicant's local time, the reason if it was deferred or skipped (quiet hours, short lead time, opted out, request already made), Twilio message id, Twilio status and error code, segment count, interview format, Indeed or non-Indeed.

Per application: job, employer, interview date, time applied, when the link was first opened and which text it followed, when a request was made and which text it followed, STOP time and the exact reply wording, and every other reply with its time and wording.

Weekly summary: applicants texted, requests and the request rate (requests divided by applicants texted), requests by the text they followed, link opens divided by delivered, delivered, undelivered and failed counts, STOP replies per text, replies forwarded, the share of texts at 4 segments, and the request rate by job, by employer, by format, and Indeed against non-Indeed.

09

Decisions to confirm

Each line is written as the spec assumes it. Scott confirms or changes the words.

  • 1. Applying two days before the interview: Text 2 lands the day before and is sent anyway, because the request is the goal.
  • 2. Text 3 for an in-person job may run to 4 segments when the title and company are long. Accept the cost rather than cut the title.
  • 3. Time zone is written as two letters (CT), not the daylight form Indeed uses (CDT).
  • 4. Text 1 on the interview morning is sent only if at least one hour remains before the closing moment.
  • 5. One confirmation text is sent after an opt-out.
  • 6. The city field holds the city only (the wording also works for "Plano, TX").
  • 7. No Simple Staffing jobs are outside the US. If any are, the time-zone table and quiet-hour rules need extending first.
