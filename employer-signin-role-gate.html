Developer notes

  • The role question appears before all three sign-in methods — Google, Microsoft and email — not just Google.
  • "I'm an employer" sets localStorage.jfxRole = 'employer' and shows the sign-in card. On later visits from that browser the question is skipped.
  • "I'm a job seeker" redirects to the job seeker sign-in page. Set the real URL in JOBSEEKER_SIGNIN_URL.
  • Backstop (recommended): a device flag can't recognise an employer on a new device or in incognito. After the provider returns, if the account already exists, sign in with no question. Only ask "employer or job seeker?" when a new employer account is about to be created.
  • Styling matches the live page: Noto Sans, 540px card, 16px radius, buttons 47px with #d4d2d0 border, primary #0044B3.
