What this list is

  • Source: the JobFairX admin, Registrations list, read on 12 October 2026. It covers 108 companies and 153 registrations for events in Canadian cities, from August 2022 to October 2026.
  • Names are shown exactly as recorded in the admin. A company can appear under more than one event type.
  • Logos: 60 files in this folder, named logo-<company>.png. Each one is a 600 by 300 pixel PNG with a transparent background, with the logo centred and given the same visual weight, so they line up in a grid with a single style rule. They come from each company's own website or Wikimedia Commons. Companies tagged Logo pick are the suggested ones to show; swap any of them.
  • Tags: Government bodies are in the lists but were left out of the logo picks. Upcoming event means the event had not taken place yet on 12 October 2026.
  • Data file: the same lists are in canada-companies.json.
  • Ready-made picture: the sixteen entry-level logos as one Past Companies picture, as used in the Burlington Eventbrite listing: past-companies-canada-entry-level.png.
