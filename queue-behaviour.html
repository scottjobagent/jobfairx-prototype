Waiting rooms and Interview rooms — how candidates move

The logic behind the event-day queue for the three locations where JobFairX cannot see the interview happening: in person, phone, and video on the employer’s own meeting link. What each state means, what moves a candidate between them, and who does the moving.

Waiting rooms and Interview rooms are two sections of the same Waiting to interview tab.
The two sections are not one list. The split is the arrival model, not video presence detection, and it means the same thing for every location. Waiting rooms = the candidate is here but you can’t take them yet. Interview rooms = you can take them right now. Merging them loses the only thing that tells an employer who they can act on.

No-show is the sixth destination and is terminal. A candidate can reach it from Waiting rooms or Interview rooms, never from Interviewing.

2The two signals that move them

Every movement through the queue is driven by two independent facts, and each one is known by exactly one party.

SignalWho knows itWhat it does
The candidate is hereCandidateMoves them out of Not yet interviewed and into Waiting rooms. In person this means “I’ve arrived”; on phone it means “I’m ready for your call.” Same signal, same destination.
An interviewer is freePlatform / EmployerPromotes the next candidate from Waiting rooms to Interview rooms. This is what makes a row actionable.
Arrival is the candidate’s to declare; starting is the employer’s. The employer never checks a candidate in — they cannot know whether someone has walked through the door or is sitting by their phone. Equally, the candidate never moves themselves into Interview rooms. Each party reports only what they can see.

Where the “interviewer is free” signal comes from

  • JobFairX video — the employer sets Your status to Available or Away in the lobby header, and the platform can also see who is inside an interview room.
  • Phone, in person, external link — there is no status control on these. Availability is inferred from what the employer is doing: anyone not currently in the Interviewing tab is free. In person adds a manual override, below.

3What the platform can actually see

This is the single fact that explains every difference between the locations.

External-link video belongs with phone and in person, not with JobFairX video. The interview happens in Teams or Zoom. JobFairX cannot see who joined, when it started, or when it finished — exactly as with a phone call or a handshake. It is a video interview by method, but an unobservable one by mechanism, and the queue has to be driven by people telling it things.

Everything below follows from that. Where the platform observes nothing, every transition needs a person to declare it, and every state needs a manual way out.

4In person

The normal path

  1. The candidate arrives at the venue and checks in Candidate. They land in Waiting rooms, status Awaiting available interviewer.
  2. An interviewer becomes free, so the candidate moves to Interview rooms, status Ready Platform.
  3. The employer collects them and presses Start interview Employer. The row moves to the Interviewing tab. No new window opens and no modal appears — the interview is happening in the room, not on the screen.
  4. The employer presses End interview, confirms, rates the candidate, and the row moves to Interviewed.

What is different about in person

The physical queue and the system queue drift apart. Someone steps out for a phone call, someone else is already sitting in front of you, a hiring manager asks for a particular candidate next. The order on screen stops matching the order in the room.

So in person — and only in person — the Waiting-rooms row menu carries Interview next. It promotes that candidate straight into Interview rooms out of turn: their status flips to Ready and the row gains the Start interview button. It is an override of the queue order, not a shortcut past a step.

5Phone

The normal path

  1. The candidate signals they are ready for the call by checking in Candidate → Waiting rooms.
  2. An interviewer becomes free → Interview rooms, status Ready Platform.
  3. The employer presses Call candidate. A panel opens beside the row with the number, a copy button and a tap-to-dial link.
  4. The employer dials from their own phone and then presses I’ve started the interview Employer → Interviewing.
  5. End interview → rate → Interviewed.
Opening the call panel changes nothing. It is not the start of the interview. Employers open it to copy a number, to check they have the right one, or to look again after leaving a voicemail. Only the explicit I’ve started the interview moves the candidate. Treating the panel as the trigger would report interviews that never happened.

Nobody answers

The most common phone failure, and it is deliberately two-strike:

  1. First No answer — the candidate goes back into the queue carrying an attempt count, and the button on their row changes to Call again. They are still a live candidate.
  2. Second No answer — now the employer is offered Mark as no-show.

Employers routinely call twice. Ending someone on the first miss would record reachable candidates as unreachable, which is a worse error than the extra click.

6Video on an external link

The normal path

  1. The employer’s meeting link is set when the event is created, and it is shown in the lobby header with a Copy link control — that is where they get it on the day.
  2. The candidate checks in Candidate → Waiting rooms. They receive the same meeting link with their confirmation.
  3. An interviewer becomes free → Interview rooms Platform.
  4. The employer starts the interview Employer → Interviewing. The conversation itself happens in Teams or Zoom, outside JobFairX.
  5. End interview → rate → Interviewed.

What is different

The queue behaves like the other two unobservable locations, and the lobby drops everything that only makes sense inside a JobFairX room:

  • No Your status control. Nothing on this screen decides when a candidate is admitted to a Teams meeting, and nothing here reaches them.
  • No Update display name. The name labels you inside the JobFairX room; the candidate will see whatever Teams shows.
  • No Test video and audio. That test exercises the JobFairX video stack, which this interview never touches.
  • The chip still reads Video. Third-party is a variant of video, not a fourth location.
Open question for the build. The row’s primary button currently behaves as it does on JobFairX video — it opens the JobFairX interview room. On an external-link event that is the wrong destination: the interview is in the employer’s meeting. Whether the button should open the meeting link, copy it, or simply move the row to Interviewing without opening anything is not decided.

7Every way out of the queue

Where the platform observes nothing, a candidate can get stuck. Nothing tells JobFairX that a phone candidate hung up, that someone in the waiting area gave up and went home, or that an interview happened entirely off-screen. So every state needs a manual exit, and the Waiting-rooms row menu is where they live.

ExitAvailable onWhat it is for
Interview nextIn person onlyPull someone forward out of queue order. Moves them to Interview rooms.
Mark as interviewedPhone, in person, external linkAn interview that happened while nobody touched the app — routine when the employer is talking rather than typing. Asks who interviewed them and the actual time, and will not confirm until both are given.
Didn’t showPhone, in person, external linkThe manual no-show, for a candidate who never turned up or stopped responding.
No answer ×2Phone onlyReached from the call panel rather than the row menu. The second one offers no-show.
This menu is not a convenience. On JobFairX video the platform can watch someone leave the room and clean up after them. On these three locations it cannot. Remove the menu and the only way a candidate ever leaves Waiting rooms is by being interviewed — which means the queue never empties and the event never reconciles.

Ending an interview, and the second option

End interview asks whether the employer has finished with that candidate:

  • Yes → the rating modal opens, pre-filled with any note already taken during the interview, and the candidate moves to Interviewed.
  • No → the candidate goes back to Waiting rooms, not to Interview rooms. They rejoin the queue rather than sitting at the front of it.
