Dev Spec — Interview Method & Location

How to use this page: open Demo states (bottom-left) — the Interview method group flips every dashboard state between the four methods. Everything you see is the spec; this panel is the written version.

1 · Data model

The interview method is set per event by the employer (one method applies to every interview at that event). Captured in the employer flow: edit-post-v3.html → Interview details, and setup-flow-v3.html step 5.

interviewMethod: 'jfx_video' | 'third_party_video' | 'phone' | 'in_person'   // default: 'jfx_video'

// third_party_video only:
meetingUrl: string (required, must start with http(s)://)

// in_person only:
address: { street*, suite, city*, state*, zip* }        // * = required
parkingInstructions: string (optional, multiline)
arrivalInstructions: string (optional, multiline)

// phone: no new fields — uses the candidate's profile phone number

All of this must flow to every candidate surface: this dashboard, confirmation email/SMS, and reminders.

2 · Method line (STATUS column, all interview states)

MethodLabel
jfx_videoVideo interview on JobFairX
third_party_videoVideo interview · employer’s link
phonePhone interview
in_personIn-person interview · {City}, {State}

At the requested stage, in_person shows city/state only plus: “You’ll get the full address and arrival details when it’s confirmed.” Full address is revealed on confirmation.

3 · ACTIONS column — confirmed state

MethodPrimaryDetails / links
jfx_videoJoin interview (disabled until start, camera icon)“Interviews are on JobFairX” · Test camera & mic · Message · Cancel · Unregister
third_party_videoJoin interview (external-link icon, opens meetingUrl in new tab)“Link provided by the employer” · no Test camera & mic · Message · Cancel · Unregister
phoneCall-out card: “{Employer} will call you at {candidate phone} at your scheduled time. No link needed — just have your phone ready.”Wrong number? Update it (→ profile) · Message · Cancel · Unregister
in_personGet directions (maps URL from address)Full address block, then collapsible View arrival details → Parking + When you arrive · Message · Cancel · Unregister

4 · Day-of (live) state

MethodState pillActions
jfx_videoIt’s time for your interviewJoin interview now + Test camera & mic
third_party_videoIt’s time for your interviewJoin interview now → meetingUrl
phoneYour interview is starting soon“Expect a call any minute from {Employer} at {phone}. Keep your phone nearby and your ringer on.”
in_personToday — head to your interviewGet directions + address + arrival details expanded by default. No join-style CTA.

In-interview state: video methods show “Return to interview” (third-party → meetingUrl); phone shows “You’re in your phone interview with {Employer}”; in-person same as live.

5 · Behavior rules

  • Test camera & mic appears only for jfx_video (it tests the JobFairX room).
  • The phone number shown is the candidate’s — employer calls candidate. “Wrong number? Update it” routes to profile.
  • Maps link: https://maps.google.com/?q={street}+{city}+{state}+{zip}
  • No changes to scheduling, cancel, or messaging flows. Candidates cannot reschedule — they accept one of the employer’s proposed times or decline — method only changes display + primary CTA.
  • Email/SMS parity: confirmations and reminders carry the same per-method content; address/parking/arrival copy comes verbatim from the employer’s Interview details.

6 · Where it lives in this file

  • METHOD global + MLBL/MICON maps, setMethod() — search “Interview method / location”.
  • Row rendering: renderConfirmed() — method × phase (before/live/joined) builds the STATUS + ACTIONS columns.
  • Requested-row line: [data-mreq] / [data-mcaveat] + updateReqM().
  • CSS: .td-method .td-call .td-addr .td-arr .arr-item .ext-ic .iv-dir
  • Employer-side capture UI: edit-post-v3.html · setup-flow-v3.html

7 · QA checklist

  • Method line shows in requested / confirmed / live / in-interview for all four methods
  • In-person hides street address until confirmed; caveat line shows at request stage
  • jfx_video experience is identical to pre-change behavior
  • Third-party join opens employer URL in new tab; no Test camera & mic
  • Phone shows candidate’s number; update-number link routes to profile
  • Get directions opens maps with the correct address; arrival details expand/collapse (auto-expanded day-of)
  • Confirmation email/SMS include per-method details
