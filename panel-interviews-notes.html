Panel interviews: several interviewers in one interview

What was added to the prototype on 6 October 2026 so that more than one interviewer can sit in the same interview with one candidate. Adding interviewers to an event already existed. This adds them to an interview. It is the employer side only, and video on JobFairX only.

Measured from Indeed's current help center, its Employer Guide, its product pages and a designer's case study of its interview room. Built as four new files on 6 October; no older page was changed and none links to these. Updated in place on 7 October (the box below).

7 Oct What changed on 7 October: the Actions menu

  • One Actions menu per row in place of the Reschedule and Cancel buttons, on the dashboard's Interviews, Upcoming list and on the lobby's upcoming list (Not yet interviewed tab). It is the Events tab's three-dot square. The menu reads Reschedule interview, Add interviewer, then a divider and Cancel interview in red. On the dashboard, phone and in-person rows have no Add interviewer (panels are video on JobFairX only); a live event's Go to Lobby rows are left alone.
  • The column heading reads Actions wherever a row offers more than one action: Upcoming, Pending and Past on the dashboard; Interviewing, Interviewed, the upcoming list and the request list in the lobby. Waiting to interview keeps Action: one button. Accept, Decline and Reschedule on a request row, and Start, Join, Rejoin, End and Leave in the lobby, stay as buttons.
  • Add interviewer in the lobby too. It opens the same dialog as the dashboard's (the event's interviewers as a checklist, then the guest link). Saving there updates the Interviewers column on the row. The dialog's "Add interviewers to the event" opens the dashboard.
  • "Add team member(s)" is now "Add interviewer" on the menu and as the dialog's title (Scott's wording; Indeed's is Add team member(s)). The two guest-link sentences keep Indeed's words.
  • Found on the live page, not fixed there: on visual-v3.html, Cancel on an Upcoming row throws a script error and its dialog never opens (openCancelInterviewModal reads .iv-review-name, which those rows do not have). The menu's Cancel interview sets up the same dialog itself.

The room opens in its own tab and the two tabs are not linked. Ending an interview inside the room does not move the row in the lobby tab. To see a row move, use End or Leave on the lobby's Interviewing tab.

2 The rules (Scott's decisions, 6 and 7 October)

  • Two ways in, as Indeed has. A colleague joins an interview already in progress from the lobby's Interviewing tab. Or team members are named on a booked interview ahead of time.
  • Who can join. Interviewers with a seat on the event join with full rights. Anyone else on the employer's team joins by a guest link with limited rights.
  • Guest rights. A guest can use chat and background. A guest cannot open the resume, take notes or give feedback. A guest does not use an interviewer seat and does not need an account.
  • At most 10 interviewers in one room. Guests count toward the 10. The candidate does not. There is only ever one candidate in a room.
  • End and Leave. The interviewer who started the interview has End, and it ends the interview for everyone. Everyone else has Leave.
  • Feedback. One Yes, Maybe or No per interviewer. Every interviewer on the interview can see all the answers. Each interviewer can change only their own. This holds for everyone, the account owner included (Scott, 7 October). On the live pages today anyone signed in can set the feedback on any row. With this change a row interviewed by someone else is fixed for you, and that includes a row still waiting for an answer.
  • Notes. Each interviewer writes their own notes. Every interviewer on the interview can read all of them. Notes are never shown to the candidate.
  • No new email. The event already has a setting for who receives updates.
  • Video on JobFairX only. No panels on phone interviews, in-person interviews, or video on the employer's own meeting link. On those, the panel code does nothing.

3 What is Indeed's and what is ours

Indeed's wording, used as it is: the button Join interview (lobby, Interviewing tab) · the guest link block's title Share a link with team members and its first two lines, from the designed version of Indeed's room · the End question and its two answers, which the prototype already had · the field label Your role in the company (Optional), which is Indeed's wording from its 2021 interviewer check-in. Indeed publishes nothing newer about that field.

What Indeed says, in our words: a panel of interviewers can join an interview in progress from the Interviewing tab. Extra interviewers can also be sent a link. Someone who joins by link has guest permission. Interviewers are asked for feedback when the interview ends. An Indeed room holds up to 50 participants, counting interviewers, guests and the one candidate.

Ours, because Indeed does not publish it: the Interviewers columns and the row states (Rejoin and Leave, Room full, "+7 more") · the counter "3 of 10 interviewers" and the "In the room" list · "This link is for this interview only." and the button name "Copy guest link" (Indeed's says "Copy link") · the menu item Add interviewer, Scott's wording for Indeed's Add team member(s) (Indeed's Interviews tab lists Join, Reschedule, Cancel, Add team member(s) and Take notes; whether they are buttons or a menu is not published) · the whole Add interviewer dialog · "This ends the interview for everyone in the room." · the line under the feedback question · notes shown under each author · the tally and its "who said what" list · "Any available interviewer" · the three messages after saving a role ("Role updated to …", "Role removed", "Display name and role updated").

Where JobFairX differs from IndeedIndeed's guest can also share a screen. The JobFairX room has no Share screen and no Record button today, so a guest gets chat and background only. If Share screen is added to the room later, guests should get it too.

The Interviewers column lists everyone in the room, in the order they joined. The first name is the one who started it. It shows three names and then "+N more", which expands the list.

Start interview (Waiting to interview tab) now moves the candidate to Interviewing with you as the one who started it. Before, the row stayed where it was. End, Yes moves the candidate to Interviewed with the room's interviewers on the row. End, No puts the candidate back at the front of the line.

Waiting to interview and the upcoming list

A read-only Interviewers column shows the team named ahead of time, or "Any available interviewer". It is a plan, not a lock: any interviewer can still press Start interview, so an absent team member never holds a candidate up. The names come from the dashboard (section 6).

Interviewed tab

  • Interviewer becomes Interviewers and sits in front of Feedback. It shows the first name and "+2" when three people interviewed.
  • One interviewer: the answer shows as before. It is a dropdown only if the answer is yours.
  • Several interviewers: one pill with a tally, such as "2 Yes, 1 Maybe" or "1 Yes, 1 not answered". Clicking it lists each interviewer with their answer. Yours is a dropdown there. A guest is listed with a Guest mark and no answer.
  • Notes opens a dialog with each interviewer's notes under their name and time, then your own box. Saving changes only yours.

Update display name (three-dot menu)

  • The dialog gains one optional field under Name: Your role in the company (Optional). The dialog title and the menu item are unchanged (Scott's pick, 7 October).
  • The role belongs to the interviewer and carries across all their events, like the display name.
  • It shows under their name in the room's "In the room" list and on their tile for the other people in the room. An empty role shows the name only.
  • Saving changes. On the live page the dialog saves only a changed name, and an unchanged name is an error. Here Save works when either the name or the role changed. An unchanged name is no longer an error. An empty name still is.

Phone and in-person events are untouched in this copy. Their row menu still carries "Add team member" from the phone build, and it still only shows a notice.

5 Room (interview-screen-v3-panel.html, a copy of interview-screen-v3.html)

  • Tiles. The candidate stays large. Your own tile stays bottom right. Each other interviewer gets a tile the same size to its left, with name and role. The role is the optional field in the lobby's Update display name dialog. A guest's tile reads Guest in place of a role. A muted interviewer shows a red mic. With many people the tiles get narrower so all of them stay in one row, long names are shortened, and your own name tag moves up above the row so nothing is covered.
  • Counter. "3 of 10 interviewers", top right. It opens "In the room": the candidate, then the interviewers in the order they joined. The one who started is marked "Started the interview". A guest is marked Guest.
  • Guest link. It sits under that list (Scott's pick). Only interviewers with a seat see it. Copy guest link copies a link to this page's guest view.
  • End or Leave. The same red button. It reads End for the one who started and Leave for everyone else.
  • Notes panel. One new line says every interviewer can read the notes. "Notes from your team" shows the others' notes under name and time. "Your notes" is your own box, saved as you type.
  • Feedback. When the interview ends, every interviewer in the room gets the existing Yes, Maybe, No question, with one new line under it. An interviewer who leaves early gets it as they leave. A guest never gets it.
  • Guest view. No Notes button, no Resume button, no guest link, no feedback question. After Leave the guest gets the existing call quality question and then "You can close this window."

6 Dashboard (visual-v3-panel.html, a copy of visual-v3.html)

Interviews, Upcoming

  • A new Interviewers column: the named team, or "Any available interviewer".
  • Every row has one Actions menu (7 October): Reschedule interview, on video rows Add interviewer, then Cancel interview. Phone and in-person rows have no Add interviewer.
  • Add interviewer opens a dialog: the interviewers on the event as a checklist with name and email, Cancel and Save. Under the list are "Not on the list? Add interviewers to the event", which opens the event's existing interviewer settings, and the guest link block.
  • Naming someone only lists them on the interview. No email is sent. On event day they join from the lobby like anyone else.
  • The lobby's upcoming list has the same menu and dialog (7 October), so the team can be named from either place.

Event Report

  • Same pattern as the lobby's Interviewed tab: Interviewers in front of Feedback, a tally for several interviewers, who said what on click, and you change only your own.
  • Filter buttons. A candidate is counted under each answer they received, so the counts can add up to more than the total. "Needs review" means at least one interviewer has not answered.
  • Interview details. "Interviewed by" lists everyone. View Notes shows each interviewer's notes under their name.
  • A no-show row is left exactly as it was.

7 What the real build needs to store

For each interviewDetail
Named teamThe interviewers picked ahead of time. A list of event interviewers. Can be empty.
Who was in the roomEach interviewer with the time they joined and left, and which one started it. Guests with the display name they typed.
FeedbackOne answer per interviewer: yes, maybe, no or not answered. Never one per interview.
NotesOne note per interviewer per candidate, with its author and time.
Guest linkOne link per interview. It works from booking until the interview ends, then stops. It needs no account.
Role (per interviewer, not per interview)Optional text, up to 40 characters, stored with the interviewer's display name and used across all their events.

Behaviour that is not visible in the prototype

  • One room at a time. An interviewer who is in a room cannot join another until they leave.
  • If the one who started drops out, End passes to the interviewer who has been in the room longest, so an interview can always be ended.
  • The 10 limit counts interviewers and guests. Join interview and the guest link both stop at 10.
  • Export. One row per candidate, with every interviewer and answer listed in that row.

8 Not built, on purpose

  • The candidate side. The only change a candidate will see is one labelled tile per interviewer in their room. No new candidate wording or steps were designed.
  • The guest's first screen. In words: the guest opens the link, types the name they want shown, and joins. If the interview has not started they wait and are let in when it does. If it has ended, or the room is full, they see a plain message.
  • Past interviews tab and Candidates page on the dashboard. They should follow the same tally. The Candidates page shows one note in the row today, so it needs a decision on how to show several authors.
  • Stepping out without ending. The one who started cannot leave without ending the interview for everyone. This is as decided. A third answer on the End question was offered and not taken.
  • Emails, healthcare copies, phone and in-person. Left out as decided.
