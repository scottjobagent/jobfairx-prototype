The employer operating system

One event. A real hiring workflow from setup to decisions.

The event dashboard is where employers post jobs, control capacity, communicate with candidates, run the live queue, and close the loop.

01 — SET UP

Post the roles you need to fill.

After registration, your team lands in one event workspace. Add healthcare jobs, assign recruiters, turn on screening, and control who receives updates.

  • ✓Keep every job, recruiter, and automation on one event
  • ✓Set resume requirements before candidates request time
  • ✓Start matching as soon as your roles are ready
02 — CONFIGURE

Choose the format and capacity for every interview.

Video, phone, or in person is a real operational choice—not a marketing preference. Select one event format, open the time slots you want, and set capacity for each.

  • ✓Video sends a secure JobFairX link automatically
  • ✓Phone shares the candidate’s number at the right time
  • ✓In person includes address, parking, and arrival instructions
03 — REVIEW & RESPOND

Every candidate arrives with the context to decide.

Open one record to see qualifications, screening answers, experience, interview status, and the conversation so far. Accept, decline, reschedule, or message without changing tools.

  • ✓Review resumes, skills, and screener responses
  • ✓Keep the full candidate conversation in context
  • ✓Automate messages at the key moments in the process
04 — EVENT IS LIVE

Your event becomes a live operating queue.

When the event starts, editing locks and the employer lobby takes over. Your team sees who is ready, who is waiting, and exactly what action to take next.

  • ✓Video: launch a private interview room
  • ✓In person: see checked-in candidates and start the meeting
  • ✓Phone: call candidates directly at their scheduled time
05 — EVENT REPORT

End with candidate decisions—not a stack of notes.

Your event report organizes every interview, interviewer, note, and outcome by candidate. Filter the list, assign feedback, schedule follow-ups, and export what your team needs.

  • ✓Separate Yes, Maybe, No, Needs review, and No-shows
  • ✓Keep interviewer notes with the candidate record
  • ✓Export an event-level hiring handoff on demand
