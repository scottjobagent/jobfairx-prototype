Notes for the developer

This page is the HubSpot Sales Dashboard as it looks on 21 Oct 2026, rebuilt one to one so the admin version looks the same, with the parts we do not need left out (last note). Every size, colour, font and spacing above was read from HubSpot's rendered page. The figures are sample numbers in HubSpot's formats, not real data.

  • Font: Lexend Deca (Google Fonts), weights 300, 400 and 600. Page background #F0F0F0, panel white with a 16px radius, cards white with a 1px #CCCCCC border and 16px radius.
  • Grid: 6 columns, 10px gutters, rows 191px tall, inside 24px padding. Monthly Sales - Daily is 3 columns wide; Sales is 3 wide and 2 rows tall, in columns 4 to 6, under Monthly Leads and the two Cycle cards. The space to its left stays empty, as on HubSpot.
  • Card: title 16px/600 in #006162 with the info icon 8px after it, pills 10px/600 on #F0F0F0 (12px apart), then the values: date 12px/600 #333333 over the number 28px/600 #006162, each row 12px apart. Lists (Daily Leads, Weekly Leads, Monthly Sales - Daily, the two Cycle cards) scroll inside the card with the wheel, trackpad or keyboard, with the browser's own scrollbar, and open at the top, as on HubSpot.
  • Change rows: a 14 x 10 triangle, green #006831 pointing up or red #AC0020 pointing down, then the percentage 14px/300. No arrow when there was nothing to compare with.
  • Sales chart: HubSpot draws it with Highcharts; the SVG above is that markup. Last year is an area in #33333399 (grey at 60%), this year a 2px line in #321986, white 3.5px markers, dashed gridlines (3,1) in the same grey, labels 11px, axis titles 12px/600. Y axis $0 to $40,000 in $10,000 steps.
  • The star, the Default tag, the gear and the filter row are part of the look only; wire them up as you see fit. HubSpot's Manage dashboards, Create dashboard, Actions, Share and Add content buttons and its Source Data card were left out on purpose (Scott, 21 Oct 2026). Times are Pacific ("Based on UTC -07:00").
  • Definitions already agreed: a lead is a contact added to ActiveCampaign; a sale is as the developer defines it; weeks run Monday to Sunday.
