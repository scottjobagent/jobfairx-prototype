Notes for the developer

Everything above this line is the page to add to the admin. The review bar at the top and these notes are not part of it.

Sales
Total sales for today, this week and this month. Use your own definition of a sale.
New leads
A lead is a contact added to ActiveCampaign. It counts on the day it is added.
Periods
Pacific time. Weeks run Monday to Sunday, the same as the HubSpot dashboard this page replaces. This week and this month are running totals up to now.
Date filter
The row under the title sets the day the whole page is shown for. Today is the default. Yesterday, End of last week and End of last month are shortcuts, and Another day takes any day from the first tracked day up to today. Every number and chart is then worked out as of the end of that day: the tiles show that day, its week up to that day and its month up to that day, the day charts show that month and that week, and Sales by month shows that year up to that day. The tile labels change from Today, This week and This month to the date, “Week of” the Monday, and the month name. A strip under the filter says which day is showing and has a Back to today link. Days before tracking started cannot be picked.
Previous period
The grey line under each number is the figure for the same point in the period before: yesterday by this time of day, last week through the same weekday and time, and last month through the same date and time. If last month is shorter than today's date, use its last day. When the date filter is on a past day, the comparison uses full days: the day before, the week before through the same weekday, and the month before through the same date. The arrow and percentage beside the number compare the two: green up, red down, rounded to a whole number. No arrow shows when the earlier figure is zero.
Day by day
Sales by day has one bar for each day of the month up to the day shown, and that day's bar carries its amount. New leads by day has one blue bar for each day of the week, with the count on top, next to a grey bar for the same day one week earlier. Pointing at a day, tapping it, or using the arrow keys shows the figures. Each chart opens as a table underneath.
Lead arrows
Under each day in New leads by day, an arrow and a percentage compare that day with the same day one week earlier: green up, red down. This is the comparison HubSpot showed in its “Leads this Cycle” and “Leads Last Cycle” tiles. The switch at the top of the chart moves between the two: this week against last week, and last week against the week before. Percentages are rounded to whole numbers. Today's arrow compares a part day with a full day, as HubSpot did. No arrow shows when the earlier day had no leads. If a percentage is too wide for its column, the arrow shows alone and the figure stays in the hover and the table.
Sales by month
This year is the solid blue line and last year is the dashed grey line. The current month is a running total and carries the dot. Pointing at a month, tapping it, or using the arrow keys shows both years. The same figures open as a table under the chart.
Year so far
Compares January 1 through the day shown with the same dates last year, so both totals cover the same number of days.
Nothing to compare yet
Tracking starts when this page goes live and no past data is loaded. Until an earlier period exists, the grey line under each number reads “No earlier data yet” and the arrow beside the number is left off, the day charts show only the days tracked so far, last week's bars, the lead arrows, the week switch and last year's line are left off, and the year row shows one total. The date filter shortcuts that reach before the first tracked day are greyed out. Choose “Day one” in the review bar to see it.
Phone
Below about 640px wide, each tile becomes one row with the arrow under the number. Below about 900px the two day charts stack. Every chart keeps the full width.
Numbers
