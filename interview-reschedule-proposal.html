Developer logic

When it appears

  • The candidate requests an interview time.
  • The employer declines that time and proposes one or more alternatives.
  • The proposal renders in the candidate's message thread for that job.

Behaviour

  • Schedule interview is disabled until a time is selected.
  • Selecting a time and submitting books the interview and posts the confirmation into the same thread.
  • Once booked, the proposal card becomes read-only — radios and the button are disabled so the candidate can't double-book.
  • None of these work pre-fills the message composer with the reply text. It does not auto-send and does not book anything.
  • The candidate cannot propose a specific time in this mode. They can only reply in free text.
  • Send a message on the confirmation focuses the composer in the same thread.

Why the copy reads this way

  • Naming the original requested time is what stops the candidate thinking "why are they sending me times when I already picked one?"
  • Company and job title appear because a candidate may have several conversations open at once.
  • The card is the loudest element in the thread — it is the only message that needs an action.
Bug found in the current build. The proposed times render in CDT but the confirmation renders in MDT. A candidate can't tell what time to show up. Times must come from one source and one timezone rule.
