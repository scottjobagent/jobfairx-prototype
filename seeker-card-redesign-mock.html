About the role

Texas Health is hiring Patient Care Technicians across its Dallas facilities. You will support nursing staff with direct patient care, vital signs, mobility assistance and daily living activities on a busy inpatient unit.

What you will do
Take and record vital signs and report changes to the charge nurse
Assist patients with bathing, dressing, mobility and meals
Keep rooms clean, stocked and ready for admissions
Document care accurately in the electronic health record
What you will bring
Current CNA or PCT certification in Texas
BLS certification, or willingness to obtain before start
Comfortable on your feet for a 12-hour shift
About Texas Health

A non-profit health system operating 29 hospitals across North Texas, with roughly 24,000 employees. Benefits include medical from day one, tuition reimbursement and shift differentials for nights and weekends.
