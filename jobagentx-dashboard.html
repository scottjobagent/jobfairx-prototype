Type: Full-time

Job Description

We are seeking a dedicated Registered Behavior Technician to deliver one-on-one ABA therapy to clients under the supervision of a Board Certified Behavior Analyst. The ideal candidate will implement treatment plans, collect data on client progress, and support skill-building in everyday settings.

Requirements

High school diploma or equivalent. Active RBT certification (or willingness to obtain). Experience working with children or in ABA therapy preferred. Strong communication skills, patience, and reliability.

Benefits

Health, dental, and vision insurance. 401(k) with company match. Paid time off and holidays. Professional development opportunities.
