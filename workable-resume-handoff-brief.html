Workable to JobFairX Resume Handoff

Bring every Workable applicant into the JobFairX admin, carry their resume onto their interview request, email them the link to request their interview time, and give the admin a Workable Candidates tab with interview-request status and conversion stats.

Brief date 18 Oct 2026 · Companion to the Simple Staffing Applicant Texts Spec (the email reuses its merge fields and send checks; its Resume step and logging line give the resume-source values) · Editable master: Claude Doc

01

Goal

When a job seeker who applied through Workable requests an interview on JobFairX, the resume they uploaded to Workable is already attached to that request, and the employer sees it without anyone forwarding a file.

Second outcome: every candidate in Workable is listed in a Workable Candidates tab of its own in the admin. They all come through Workable but start on different boards, so each row carries a column for where they came from (Indeed, Monster, a free job board, whatever it was), a column that fills once they request an interview, and the stats and conversions for that population live on the same page (Scott, 18 Oct 2026).

Third outcome: every applicant also gets one email with the link to request their interview time, from scott@jobfairx.com. Replies are welcome and go to a folder Scott checks in that inbox (Scott, 18 Oct 2026).

In scope: every applicant to a job JobFairX posts through Workable, whichever board they applied from (Workable's free boards or the sponsored Indeed listing). Out of scope: how jobs are posted, and anything on Indeed's side. Workable is the only system the developer integrates with.

02

What exists today

The Workable webhook is already live in Brandon's code: when someone applies to a job posted through Workable, Workable posts the candidate to JobFairX and that handler creates Text 1. The payload already carries the resume link (confirmed read-only on 17 Oct 2026). Nothing in this brief needs a new connection to Workable; it extends what the webhook does with each candidate.

The flow as it runs now:

  1. Simple Staffing posts a client employer's job through Workable. Workable pushes it to free job boards and Simple Staffing sponsors it on Indeed.
  2. The applicant applies on whichever board, with a resume (21 of 22 current jobs require one). The application lands in Workable as a candidate on that job.
  3. Workable fires the webhook. JobFairX texts the applicant 15 minutes later with a schedule link ("Request your interview time on JobFairX"). Texts 2 and 3 follow on later days if no request comes.
  4. On the request page the applicant picks a time, answers the screener, provides a resume (the step is hidden when the employer marks it not required) and creates an account. The employer accepts or declines, usually within 24 hours.
  5. The resume steps are the biggest drop-off: 30 of 64 quits between 8 and 17 Oct 2026. The conversion plan Scott approved on 17 Oct (change 2) already says the app attaches the Workable resume and the employer's requirement stays. This brief is the build detail for that change plus the new admin tab.

What the admin shows today: a text log per application, the SMS Indeed Conversion screen (texted, started, finished, quits by step, 10-day window) and each job panel's registered and request counts. Brandon's year export (17 Oct 2025 to 17 Oct 2026) holds 8,262 texted applications with the text each request followed, so the data for the new tab's stats already exists in the database.

03

Recommended design

Extend the existing webhook handler, then build the tab on the same rows. No new Workable connection and no link tracking.

Every delivery passes through the one handler. From there the same candidate row feeds the applicant email, the request page (which attaches the resume) and the tab (which fills the Requested column and counts the stats).

  1. Store the candidate, not only the text job. On each candidate-created delivery, save or update a Workable candidate row in JobFairX: Workable candidate ID, job shortcode and the JobFairX job it maps to, name, email, phone, the board they came from (Workable's domain field, which also fixes today's "indeed: unknown" on 5,336 of 8,262 rows), applied time, resume filename and type, and no parsed profile for now (decision 2, 18 Oct). Brandon's applications table already holds the job, employer, applied time, texts and request columns, so this is a handful of new fields on that row or a sibling table keyed by application.
  2. Download the resume at once and keep it. Fetch the file at the resume link in the payload and store it in JobFairX's own file storage, the same place uploaded resumes live, linked to the candidate row. Do it in the webhook job with a retry queue, because Workable's docs do not say how long the link stays valid. If the payload has no resume link, call the candidate endpoint once by ID; if there is still none, mark the row "no resume".
  3. Attach at request time by matching, not by a tracked link. The links stay exactly as the texts spec has them, and this build adds no link tracking (Scott's rule, 17 Oct 2026). When the applicant requests a time for that job and enters their phone or email, the app looks for a Workable candidate on the same job with the same phone (normalized to E.164) or the same email (case-insensitive). Brandon already credits requests to texted applications (the export's request time and request text columns), so this reuses that match. On a match, the app attaches that file to the request and skips the Upload a resume step and the resume builder, as the spec's Resume step says. The confirmation shows the filename so the applicant knows which resume the employer gets. When the employer does not require a resume, the step stays hidden as today. The request records resume source = Workable, and the employer sees the resume on the interview request exactly as an uploaded one.
  4. Email the applicant their link. The same background job sends one email per application from scott@jobfairx.com, a few minutes after applying and ahead of Text 1. Replies go to a folder Scott checks (Applicant email, below).
  5. The tab reads the same rows. Every Workable candidate row is a line in the Workable Candidates tab, the request match fills its Requested column, and the stats are counts over those rows.

If no candidate matches (a different phone and email), the applicant uploads a resume as today and the request is recorded with resume source = uploaded. That case is visible in the tab's stats, so it stays measurable.

Workable Candidates tab (admin)

Open the Workable Candidates tab design (new window)

A new tab of its own in the admin, not a section under Manage Candidates. Scott's definition (18 Oct 2026): "Those are all the Workable candidates. Anyone that submitted a resume from our job posting, and those are the ones we want to convert to request interviews." Every row is marked as from Workable, and the page carries the stats and conversions for this population.

Who is in it. Every candidate Workable sends for our postings (applied, not recruiter-sourced), from the day the tab goes live, moving forward. No backfill (Scott, 18 Oct 2026). A candidate Workable delivered without a resume file stays in the list with Resume shown as None, so the count matches Workable (decision 4, confirmed by Scott 18 Oct 2026: every applicant).

Stats and conversions on the page. Counted over the same rows the filters select, so any slice (one job, one employer, one month) shows its own conversion:

  • Candidates, and how many have a resume
  • Texted (Text 1 delivered)
  • Requested an interview, and the request rate = requested ÷ candidates. This is the conversion Scott tracks (39% over the past year, 29% in October 2026 per Brandon's export)
  • Requested with the Workable resume, an uploaded file, a built resume, or skipped (the spec's resume-source values). This shows whether attaching the Workable resume moves the number
  • Median minutes from Text 1 to request
  • Which message each request followed (the email, Text 1, 2 or 3), credited to the last one sent before the request, as the export does today for texts
  • A weekly trend of candidates, requests and request rate over a period the admin picks (Scott's standing ask: wider windows than the 10 days the SMS Indeed Conversion screen shows)
  • A breakdown table by job, employer and source: candidates, requested, rate, sortable

With no backfill the stats start at go-live. The check: for any day after go-live, the tab's candidates and requests equal the SMS Indeed Conversion screen's texted and requests for that day.

07

Applicant email

Every applicant gets one email with the link to request their interview time, sent from scott@jobfairx.com. Replies are welcome and land in a folder Scott checks in that inbox (Scott, 18 Oct 2026). JobFairX sends it, not Workable.

Why not Workable. Workable's email placeholders cover the candidate's name, the job title, the account name (Simple Staffing) and Workable's own links, such as its self-schedule tool. None can hold the JobFairX link, the employer or the interview date, and custom placeholders in curly brackets are typed in by hand before each send, so they cannot work in an automation (Managing communication templates). JobFairX holds every one of these values when it prepares Text 1.

Sender and replies

SettingValue
Fromscott@jobfairx.com, sender name Simple Staffing (decision 9)

Reply folderA Gmail filter in scott@jobfairx.com: mail to scott+applicants@jobfairx.com gets the label Applicant Replies, skips the inbox and never goes to spam. Set up in Scott's Gmail, not in code

When it goes. Once per application, from the background job that schedules Text 1, as soon as the candidate row is stored: a few minutes after applying, at any hour. The applicant gets Workable's own confirmation first, then this email, then Text 1 fifteen minutes after applying. Texts 2 and 3 stay texts, and there are no reminder emails.

When it does not go. When the texts spec's send checks would hold a text: slots full, job closed or paused, interviews begun, or the date passed. Also when the Workable job has no JobFairX job, the candidate has no email address, or a recruiter added the candidate.

Words. A plain letter: a text part and a matching simple HTML part, no logo, images or button, with the link written out in full. The merge fields are Text 1's (texts spec, Merge fields), with " on Indeed" only for Indeed applicants. {Job title} is the title on the Indeed post, which is the Workable job title, so take it from Workable: the JobFairX title differs on some jobs.

Subject: Request your interview time: {Job title} with {Company}

Hi {First name},

This is Scott with Simple Staffing. We received your application for the {Job title} job on Indeed.

{Company} would like to interview you on {Interview date} between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM {Time zone}. {Format sentence}

{Request line}

Simple Staffing will also text you from (762) 465-9036 with a link to request your time.

If you have a question, reply to this email.

Thanks,
Scott
Simple Staffing
simplestaffing.co

{Format sentence}, {Request line} and {Schedule link} come from the texts spec; for an in-person job the two lines read "Interviews are in person at their location in {City}." and "Request your interview time on JobFairX: {Schedule link}". Leave out the line about the text when no Text 1 will go to that applicant (no phone that takes texts, or STOP on that number). Scott may change the words; the fields stay.

Logging and the tab. Log on the application: send time, SendGrid message ID and outcome (delivered, bounced, dropped, or skipped with the reason). The detail drawer in the Workable Candidates tab shows the email beside the texts. Requests stay credited to the last message sent before them, with the email as one more message: a request between the email and Text 1 counts for the email.

Canadian applicants get the email too, on the basis the texts spec gives for the texts under Canada's anti-spam law.

08

Build steps in order

Steps 2 to 5 ship first, because they move the conversion. The tab (step 6) follows on the same rows.

  1. Access and subscription (checked 18 Oct 2026). The token has r_candidates and r_jobs and is valid until 21 July 2027, and the webhook delivers within a minute for every job that had applicants (Workable API facts, above). Brandon only confirms that the handler verifies the signature header.
  2. Store candidates and resumes (Brandon). Add the fields in the data table, upsert on every delivery keyed by Workable candidate ID, answer 200 before doing the work, and run the resume download in a background job with retries. Set resume_status on every row.
  3. No backfill (Scott, 18 Oct). The tab starts at go-live and moves forward. Nothing historical is pulled.
  4. Attach at request time (Brandon). Match the requester to a Workable candidate on the same job by phone, then email. Attach the file, skip the Upload a resume step and the resume builder, show the filename on the confirmation, record the resume source with the spec's four values, and add the "From Workable application" label on the employer's view.
  5. Applicant email (Scott's Gmail filter, then Brandon). The Applicant Replies label and filter go into Scott's Gmail first. Then send the email from the webhook job with the sender, Reply-To and checks in the Applicant email section, log it, and show it in the drawer.
  6. Workable Candidates tab (Brandon). The table with its filters, the detail drawer, the Requested column and the Source badge first. Then the stats row, the weekly trend, the breakdown table, the collapsible stats block and the fixed blue header row.
  7. Reconcile (Brandon, Scott checks). For any day after go-live, the tab's candidates and requests must match the SMS Indeed Conversion screen's texted and requests for that day.
  8. Monitor (Brandon). Alert on webhook deliveries that fail or replay, on resume downloads still failing after the retries, on emails SendGrid rejects, on a 401 from Workable, and on a daily gap between candidates Workable created and rows upserted.

10

Privacy and applicant-facing copy

The applicant gave the resume to apply for this job. Attaching it to the interview request for the same job and the same employer is the purpose they gave it for. Say so anyway, in two places:

  • On the request confirmation: "Resume: {filename}, from your application. The employer sees it with your request." Approved by Scott, 18 Oct 2026.
  • In the JobFairX privacy policy, one added sentence: "Applications received through Simple Staffing's postings on Workable, including the resume, are shared with the employer interviewing for that job." Approved by Scott, 18 Oct 2026.

The texts stay exactly as the approved spec has them; the only other new words an applicant sees are in the Applicant email section. Store the files with the same access controls as uploaded resumes, and keep them indefinitely, Workable deletions not mirrored (decision 5, Scott 18 Oct 2026); a deletion request that reaches JobFairX directly is handled as the privacy policy says today. Canadian applicants (785 of the year's 8,262 rows) are handled the same way; the privacy-policy sentence covers the stated purpose for them too.

Acceptance criteria

  • A new Workable applicant appears in the Workable Candidates tab within 5 minutes of applying, with name, job, board, applied time and resume filename, and the file opens from the row.
  • A replayed webhook delivery creates no second row, no second text and no second email.
  • A candidate whose Workable application had no resume shows Resume: None and reaches today's upload step when requesting.
  • An applicant who requests a time on the texted job, using the texted phone or the applied email, sees no Upload a resume step and no resume builder. The employer opens the request and sees the Workable file labelled "From Workable application".
  • On a job marked resume not required, the steps stay hidden and the file is still on the request (decision 3).
  • The Requested column fills with the request time within a minute of the request and links to it.
  • For any day after go-live, the tab's candidates and requests match the SMS Indeed Conversion screen for that day.
  • Filtering by one job shows that job's candidates, requests and rate, and the breakdown table sums to the totals.
  • From go-live, every candidate Workable creates appears as a row the same day, and nothing historical is pulled.
  • The build adds no link tracking: the links and the texts stay as the texts spec has them.
  • Alerts fire on a download still failing after the retries and on a webhook response other than 200.
  • A new applicant on a job with a JobFairX job gets one email within 5 minutes of applying, from scott@jobfairx.com with Reply-To scott+applicants@jobfairx.com, naming the Workable job title, the employer, the interview date and window, with the request link. Gmail's Show original reports SPF, DKIM and DMARC as PASS.
  • A reply to that email lands under the Applicant Replies label in scott@jobfairx.com, not in the inbox.
  • No email goes when the job has no JobFairX job, when its slots are full or its date has passed, or when the candidate has no email address.
