Workable to JobFairX Resume Handoff

Bring every Workable applicant into the JobFairX admin, carry their resume onto their interview request, and give the admin a Workable Candidates tab with interview-request status and conversion stats.

01

Goal

When a job seeker who applied through Workable requests an interview on JobFairX, the resume they uploaded to Workable is already attached to that request, and the employer sees it without anyone forwarding a file.

Second outcome: every candidate in Workable is listed in a Workable Candidates tab of its own in the admin. They all come through Workable but start on different boards, so each row carries a column for where they came from (Indeed, Monster, a free job board, whatever it was), a column that fills once they request an interview, and the stats and conversions for that population live on the same page (Scott, 18 Oct 2026).

In scope: every applicant to a job JobFairX posts through Workable, whichever board they applied from (Workable's free boards or the sponsored Indeed listing). Out of scope: how jobs are posted, and anything on Indeed's side. Workable is the only system the developer integrates with.

02

What exists today

The Workable webhook is already live in Brandon's code: when someone applies to a job posted through Workable, Workable posts the candidate to JobFairX and that handler creates Text 1. The payload already carries the resume link (confirmed read-only on 17 Oct 2026). Nothing in this brief needs a new connection to Workable; it extends what the webhook does with each candidate.

The flow as it runs now:

  1. Simple Staffing posts a client employer's job through Workable. Workable pushes it to free job boards and Simple Staffing sponsors it on Indeed.
  2. The applicant applies on whichever board, with a resume (21 of 22 current jobs require one). The application lands in Workable as a candidate on that job.
  3. Workable fires the webhook. JobFairX texts the applicant 15 minutes later with the job's schedule link ("Request your interview time on JobFairX"). Texts 2 and 3 follow on later days if no request comes.
  4. On the request page the applicant picks a time, answers the screener, provides a resume (the step is hidden when the employer marks it not required) and creates an account. The employer accepts or declines, usually within 24 hours.
  5. The resume steps are the biggest drop-off: 30 of 64 quits between 8 and 17 Oct 2026. The conversion plan Scott approved on 17 Oct (change 2) already says the app attaches the Workable resume and the employer's requirement stays. This brief is the build detail for that change plus the new admin tab.

What the admin shows today: a text log per application, the SMS Indeed Conversion screen (texted, started, finished, quits by step, 10-day window) and each job panel's registered and request counts. Brandon's year export (17 Oct 2025 to 17 Oct 2026) holds 8,262 texted applications with the text each request followed, so the data for the new tab's stats already exists in the database.

03

Recommended design

Extend the existing webhook handler in three places, then build the tab on the same rows. No new Workable connection, no per-applicant link codes.

Every delivery passes through the one handler. From there the same candidate row serves the request page, which attaches the resume, and the tab, which fills the Requested column and counts the stats.

  1. Store the candidate, not only the text job. On each candidate-created delivery, save or update a Workable candidate row in JobFairX: Workable candidate ID, job shortcode and the JobFairX job it maps to, name, email, phone, the board they came from (Workable's domain field, which also fixes today's "indeed: unknown" on 5,336 of 8,262 rows), applied time, resume filename and type, and no parsed profile for now (decision 2, 18 Oct). Brandon's applications table already holds the job, employer, applied time, texts and request columns, so this is a handful of new fields on that row or a sibling table keyed by application.
  2. Download the resume at once and keep it. Fetch the file at the resume link in the payload and store it in JobFairX's own file storage, the same place uploaded resumes live, linked to the candidate row. Do it in the webhook job with a retry queue, because Workable's docs do not say how long the link stays valid. If the payload has no resume link, call the candidate endpoint once by ID; if there is still none, mark the row "no resume".
  3. Attach at request time by matching, not by a tracked link. The schedule link stays the job's plain link (Scott's rule, 17 Oct 2026: no link codes or click tracking). When the applicant requests a time for that job and enters their phone or email, the app looks for a Workable candidate on the same job with the same phone (normalized to E.164) or the same email (case-insensitive). Brandon already credits requests to texted applications (the export's request time and request text columns), so this reuses that match. On a match, the app attaches that file to the request and skips the Upload a resume step and the resume builder, as the spec's Resume step says. The confirmation shows the filename so the applicant knows which resume the employer gets. When the employer does not require a resume, the step stays hidden as today. The request records resume source = Workable, and the employer sees the resume on the interview request exactly as an uploaded one.
  4. The tab reads the same rows. Every Workable candidate row is a line in the Workable Candidates tab, the request match fills its Requested column, and the stats are counts over those rows.

If no candidate matches (a different phone and email), the applicant uploads a resume as today and the request is recorded with resume source = uploaded. That case is visible in the tab's stats, so it stays measurable.

05

Changes on the JobFairX side

Webhook handler. Keep the Text 1 scheduling as it is. Add: verify the signature header, answer 200 at once and do the rest in a background job, upsert the candidate row keyed by Workable candidate ID (replays then do nothing twice), and queue the resume download.

Data. One Workable candidate per application, on the applications row or a sibling table keyed by application_id:

FieldComes fromNotes
workable_candidate_idpayload idUnique. The idempotency key for replays
workable_job_shortcodepayload job.shortcodeMaps to the JobFairX job_id
first_name, last_name, email, phonepayloadPhone normalized to E.164, email lower-cased. These drive the request match

applied_atpayload created_atAlready present as applied_utc

resume_statusappstored, no resume, or download failed (retrying)

resume_source (on the request)appattached from Workable, uploaded, built, or skipped (the spec's four values)

Request flow. On a job where the applicant matches a Workable candidate, the app attaches the Workable resume and skips the Upload a resume step and the resume builder (the spec's Resume step). The confirmation shows the filename. When the employer does not require a resume the step stays hidden, as Scott ruled on 17 Oct, and the Workable resume is still attached to the request so the employer has it (decision 3, confirmed by Scott 18 Oct 2026).

Employer view. The resume appears on the interview request exactly where an uploaded one appears, with a small "From Workable application" label and the filename. No parsed profile for now (decision 2, 18 Oct 2026): the file is what the employer sees.

Backfill. None. Scott (18 Oct 2026): no backfill, moving forward only. The tab, the stored resumes and the stats start at go-live. If that ever changes, the list endpoint with created_after plus a fetch by ID for each resume link does it at one request per second.

06

Workable Candidates tab (admin)

Open the Workable Candidates tab design (new window)

A new tab of its own in the admin, not a section under Manage Candidates. Scott's definition (18 Oct 2026): "Those are all the Workable candidates. Anyone that submitted a resume from our job posting, and those are the ones we want to convert to request interviews." Every row is marked as from Workable, and the page carries the stats and conversions for this population.

Who is in it. Every candidate Workable sends for our postings (applied, not recruiter-sourced), from the day the tab goes live, moving forward. No backfill (Scott, 18 Oct 2026). A candidate Workable delivered without a resume file stays in the list with Resume shown as None, so the count matches Workable (decision 4, confirmed by Scott 18 Oct 2026: every applicant).

Filters: applied date range, job, employer, interview date, source, requested (yes / no), resume (yes / no), country (US / CA). Search by name, email or phone. Default sort: newest applied first. No export from the admin (Scott, 18 Oct 2026). A single-day filter sits in the period row beside the preset ranges, always visible, not behind a Custom button.

Layout rules from Scott (18 Oct 2026): no Export CSV or Columns buttons on the page. Desktop only: the admin is used on desktop, so no phone layout is needed. The stats block collapses and expands with one button, remembered per admin user, so the list comes up. The table's blue header row (Candidate, Job, Interview, Applied, Source and the rest) stays fixed at the top of the screen while the list scrolls. Scott's private working copy of the same screen: Workable Candidates Tab.

Stats and conversions on the page. Counted over the same rows the filters select, so any slice (one job, one employer, one month) shows its own conversion:

  • Candidates, and how many have a resume
  • Texted (Text 1 delivered)
  • Requested an interview, and the request rate = requested ÷ candidates. This is the conversion Scott tracks (39% over the past year, 29% in October 2026 per Brandon's export)
  • Requested with the Workable resume, an uploaded file, a built resume, or skipped (the spec's resume-source values). This shows whether attaching the Workable resume moves the number
  • Median minutes from Text 1 to request
  • Which text each request followed (Text 1, 2 or 3), credited to the last text sent before the request, as the export does today
  • A weekly trend of candidates, requests and request rate over a period the admin picks (Scott's standing ask: wider windows than the 10 days the SMS Indeed Conversion screen shows)
  • A breakdown table by job, employer and source: candidates, requested, rate, sortable

With no backfill the stats start at go-live. The check: for any day after go-live, the tab's candidates and requests equal the SMS Indeed Conversion screen's texted and requests for that day.

07

Build steps in order

Steps 2 to 4 ship first, because they move the conversion. The tab (step 5) follows on the same rows.

  1. Access and subscription (Scott, then Brandon). Confirm the Workable access token has r_candidates and r_jobs and note its expiry date. Check the live subscription covers all jobs (job_shortcode and stage_slug set to empty strings) and that the handler verifies the signature header.
  2. Store candidates and resumes (Brandon). Add the fields in the data table, upsert on every delivery keyed by Workable candidate ID, answer 200 before doing the work, and run the resume download in a background job with retries. Set resume_status on every row.
  3. No backfill (Scott, 18 Oct). The tab starts at go-live and moves forward. Nothing historical is pulled.
  4. Attach at request time (Brandon). Match the requester to a Workable candidate on the same job by phone, then email. Attach the file, skip the Upload a resume step and the resume builder, show the filename on the confirmation, record the resume source with the spec's four values, and add the "From Workable application" label on the employer's view.
  5. Workable Candidates tab (Brandon). The table with its filters, the detail drawer, the Requested column and the Source badge first. Then the stats row, the weekly trend, the breakdown table, the collapsible stats block and the fixed blue header row.
  6. Reconcile (Brandon, Scott checks). For any day after go-live, the tab's candidates and requests must match the SMS Indeed Conversion screen's texted and requests for that day.
  7. Monitor (Brandon). Alert on webhook deliveries that fail or replay, on resume downloads still failing after the retries, and on a daily gap between candidates Workable created and rows upserted.

Edge cases and failure handling

CaseHandling
Replayed or duplicate deliveryUpsert by Workable candidate ID. A replay changes nothing, sends no second text, and still gets a 200
Payload without a resume linkFetch the candidate by ID once. Still none: resume_status = no resume, and the request flow falls back to today's upload step
Resume download fails (expired link, server error, timeout)Retry with backoff for 24 hours, then refetch the candidate by ID for a fresh link. After that, mark download failed and alert. The row stays in the tab
File type and sizeAccept pdf, doc, docx and rtf. Store the file as received; convert for preview only if the current resume viewer needs it. Apply the same size cap and virus scan as uploads today ([PLACEHOLDER: current upload limit])
Same person, several jobsOne row per application, as Workable has it. The match runs within the job on the schedule link, so the right application is credited
Requester uses a different phone and emailNo match. Today's upload step runs, resume source = uploaded or built, and that candidate's Requested column stays empty. The gap shows in the stats
Phone formatsNormalize both sides to E.164 before comparing. US and Canadian numbers share +1
Recruiter-sourced candidates (sourced = true)Not applicants. Keep them out of the tab and the texts
Candidate copied or moved between jobs in WorkableTreat as a new application row. Keep the original's resume if the new payload lacks one
Candidate updates the resume in Workable laterNo event announces it. Ignore it; the request carries the file as of the application
Candidate deleted in Workable (candidate_deleted event)Ignored (decision 5, Scott 18 Oct 2026): no subscription to candidate_deleted; the row and the file stay. A deletion request that reaches JobFairX directly is handled as the privacy policy says today
Rate limit (HTTP 429)Sleep and retry. With no backfill, only the occasional fetch by ID calls the API, far under the limit
Token expiryRenewal keeps the same token string. Calendar the date and alert on a 401 from Workable

09

Privacy and applicant-facing copy

The applicant gave the resume to apply for this job. Attaching it to the interview request for the same job and the same employer is the purpose they gave it for. Say so anyway, in two places:

  • On the request confirmation: "Resume: {filename}, from your application. The employer sees it with your request." Approved by Scott, 18 Oct 2026.
  • In the JobFairX privacy policy, one added sentence: "Applications received through Simple Staffing's postings on Workable, including the resume, are shared with the employer interviewing for that job." Approved by Scott, 18 Oct 2026.

The texts stay exactly as the approved spec has them; this build adds no text copy. Store the files with the same access controls as uploaded resumes, and keep them indefinitely, Workable deletions not mirrored (decision 5, Scott 18 Oct 2026); a deletion request that reaches JobFairX directly is handled as the privacy policy says today. Canadian applicants (785 of the year's 8,262 rows) are handled the same way; the privacy-policy sentence covers the stated purpose for them too.

11

Acceptance criteria

  • A new Workable applicant appears in the Workable Candidates tab within 5 minutes of applying, with name, job, board, applied time and resume filename, and the file opens from the row.
  • A replayed webhook delivery creates no second row and no second text.
  • A candidate whose Workable application had no resume shows Resume: None and reaches today's upload step when requesting.
  • An applicant who requests a time on the texted job, using the texted phone or the applied email, sees no Upload a resume step and no resume builder. The employer opens the request and sees the Workable file labelled "From Workable application".
  • On a job marked resume not required, the steps stay hidden and the file is still on the request (decision 3).
  • The Requested column fills with the request time within a minute of the request and links to it.
  • For any day after go-live, the tab's candidates and requests match the SMS Indeed Conversion screen for that day.
  • Filtering by one job shows that job's candidates, requests and rate, and the breakdown table sums to the totals.
  • From go-live, every candidate Workable creates appears as a row the same day, and nothing historical is pulled.
  • The schedule link carries no per-applicant code and the texts are unchanged.
  • Alerts fire on a download still failing after the retries and on a webhook response other than 200.
